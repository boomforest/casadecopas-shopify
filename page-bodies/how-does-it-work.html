WHERE THE MONEY GOES

Every dollar above a vendor's pay goes into the fund. Artists keep 100% of their masters and publishing. We hold Candid's Seal of Transparency.

QUESTIONS

How are grants chosen?
Grant pools are weighted by quality of art, amount of support and length of membership: a moving pool that reaches every part of the community.
Can I buy my way into the House of Cups?
No. Only service crosses.
What is Love?
Your standing in the house: one Love per dollar you bring into the program. It never expires and is never spent.
Are minors safe here?
Ages 13 to 18 join through a parent or guardian, who checks in with them at every Casa activity.
